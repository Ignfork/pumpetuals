06 · SCORINGFAIR, PUBLIC, PRICE-ONLY

THE MATH IS THE REFEREE.

Bold calls that land score more. Safe calls that land still score. Calls that hit their stop lose the points at risk.

points = 100 × progress × reach × time × conviction
target hit → progress 1.0 + 50% bonus · stop hit → −100 × conviction · expiry → progress as-is
PROGRESS−1 → 1

How far price moved from entry toward your target.

REACH×0.5 → ×3

Target distance in %. Calling +10% beats calling +1%.

TIME×1 → ×2

1h ×1.0 · 4h ×1.2 · 24h ×1.5 · 7d ×2.0

CONVICTION×1 → ×3

More at risk, more to win. Also scales the stop-out loss.
